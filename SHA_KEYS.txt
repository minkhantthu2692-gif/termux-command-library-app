KEYSTORE BACKUP
---------------
1. Copy the .jks and its credentials file into an encrypted archive.
2. Store the archive on two separate offline media in different locations.
3. Record the SHA-256 checksum of the .jks next to the archive.
4. Restore-test the backup in a temporary folder and confirm the fingerprints
   above with: keytool -list -v -keystore <restored file> -alias main-upload
5. Losing this key permanently prevents updates under the same signing identity.

NOTE ON PLAY APP SIGNING
------------------------
If Play App Signing is enabled, Google re-signs the bundle with its own app
signing key. Register that key's SHA-1/SHA-256 in Firebase as well, otherwise
Google Sign-In fails in Play-distributed builds.
